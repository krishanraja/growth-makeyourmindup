# Job: the day's pack (Mon, Wed, Fri, after the piece is out)

Kind `daily`. Engines 2, 3 and 6 in `PLAYBOOK.md`. Time for Krish: about
10 minutes.

1. `python3 growth.py pieces --days 2 --full`. Take today's article (the
   "VIDEO:" post is the same story). If nothing is out yet, post a pack with
   headline "No piece out yet today" and no items, and stop.
2. Read it whole. Write down, before drafting:
   - **the forwardable unit**: the one finding, chart, quote or dated call a
     stranger would send on;
   - the two or three most surprising named facts;
   - **who has a reason to share it**: each person or company named in it,
     and anyone whose work it builds on;
   - its **breakout score** (`PLAYBOOK.md`), out of 20, with the weakest two.
     Below 15: draft a lighter pack (LinkedIn, X and the search fixes only)
     and say why in the headline.
3. Draft, one item each. Every makeyourmindup link is tracked
   (`python3 growth.py link`). Every draft keeps the idea the same and
   changes the hook, pace, proof and ask to suit the platform. Never paste
   the same text twice.
   - **LinkedIn post** (`linkedin`). The tension or outcome in the first two
     lines, first person, with the topic word in line one (`hooks.json`).
     Then the finding, 3 to 5 useful points, what the reader should do, and
     an ask matched to the topic. Under 1,300 characters, link in the body.
   - **LinkedIn document outline** (`linkedin`, only on the piece of the week
     with the highest breakout score): 6 to 8 slides, a title and one line
     each, for Krish to make into a carousel.
   - **X thread** (`x`): 4 to 6 posts separated by blank lines, under 280
     characters each. Hook, stakes for a named reader, evidence, the
     finding, what it means, an open loop, then a specific line to
     subscribe with the link in the last post. Post 1 stands alone.
   - **YouTube** (`youtube`), if the video is up: a pinned comment (a
     question that starts discussion, then the tracked link) and a community
     post (one line on the finding plus a poll question with 2 to 4 answers).
     The description is on the publish board already: don't redo it.
   - **Instagram Story sequence** (`instagram`): 3 to 4 frames, as text
     lines: tension, insight, proof, then the link sticker's words and the
     tracked link.
   - **Shorts and TikTok closing line** (`tiktok`): one spoken sentence of
     at most 15 words that names "makeyourmindup" aloud. Links in Shorts
     aren't clickable, so the name has to do the work.
   - **Spotify** (`spotify`), once the show exists: show notes (two lines on
     the finding, the tracked link, "best place to start" if it is the
     strongest episode) and a clip caption.
   - **Search fixes** (`seo`), two items: an SEO title of 51 to 60
     characters that matches the article's title with the main search words
     first, plus a meta description under 155 characters; and a
     two-sentence answer for the top of the article that AI answer engines
     can quote.
   - **Partner kit** (`partner`), one per person or company with a reason to
     share: a short note to them (`target_name`, `target_url` = where to
     reach them publicly), the line about them in the piece, a quote they
     could post, and a tracked link. Never claim they endorsed it.
4. Last Friday of the month: add a **calls record** post (`linkedin`):
   which dated calls fell due and whether each came true, only from a public
   source. Otherwise say "not due yet" and what is coming.
5. Headline: the day, the piece and the score, for example "Wednesday, Muse
   (16/20): LinkedIn, X thread, Story, pinned comment, 2 search fixes, 2
   partner kits". Check, then post.
