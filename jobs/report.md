# Job: the weekly report (Sunday, after numbers)

Kind `report`. Time for Krish: about 15 minutes.

1. `python3 growth.py read --days 8`: the week's packs, with
   what he marked Posted or Skipped, and today's numbers.
2. `python3 growth.py pieces --days 8`: what was published.
3. Write the report as items (`platform: other`), each a short section:
   - **Numbers**: each platform against last week, from the numbers packs
     only. Say "not shown" where a number is missing. No percentages.
   - **What pulled people in**: the pieces, posts and replies that came
     before a jump, and which drafts he actually posted.
   - **What to stop**: a job he skipped all week, or that moved nothing.
   - **Next week**: targets (start: 5 radar replies a day, 3 pitches, 3 to 5
     swap asks) and any change you recommend to a job, for him to keep or
     kill.
   - **Topics that pulled people in**: 3 lines he can take into planning
     next week's pieces.
4. Headline: the week in one plain line. Check, then post.
