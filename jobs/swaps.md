# Job: recommendation swaps (Thursday)

Kind `swaps`. Time for Krish: about 10 minutes (he sends 3 to 5).

Substack recommendations are the biggest free source of new subscribers.
A swap: he recommends their Substack, they recommend his.

1. Read `lists/swaps.md`. Skip anyone asked in the last 60 days.
2. Find 3 to 5 Substacks on AI, the creator economy, media and adtech, or the
   business of tech, of a similar size to his or a bit bigger, that posted
   in the last two weeks. Read one of their recent posts.
3. For each, one item (`platform: substack`): `target_url` (their
   Substack), `target_name` (the writer, and subscribers if shown), `why`
   (the overlap in one line), `text`: a note under 90 words that names their
   post and one thing he liked in it, says what makeyourmindup is in one
   line, and offers a recommendation swap. Plain and warm, no flattery.
4. If a Gmail tool is connected and the writer shows a public email, also
   save a Gmail **draft** (subject starts "[makeyourmindup]"). Never send.
5. Final message: the new rows for `lists/swaps.md`. Headline: "Swaps: 4
   Substacks to ask this week". Check, then post.
