# Browser access: read with Krish's logins, never act

Krish runs this engine with a browser that is signed in to his channels. That
lets a job read what only he can see: dashboards, his own feed, sources of
subscribers. The browser is for **reading only**.

## The line you never cross

In a signed-in browser you may open pages, scroll, search, sort and filter,
open analytics, and copy text out. You may **never**:

- type into a post, comment, reply, message, note or chat box (not even to
  "prepare" a draft there; drafts live in the pack);
- press Post, Publish, Send, Reply, Like, Repost, Restack, Follow, Connect,
  Subscribe, Join, Save, Schedule, Boost, Promote or Pay;
- change a setting, a profile, a bio, a link, a welcome page, an email, a
  recommendation, a referral programme or a price;
- accept or decline anything (invitations, cookies beyond what is needed to
  read, terms), or sign in to an account Krish is not already signed in to.

If a page shows a pop-up that needs one of these, close it or leave the page.
If a task seems to need one, write it as a draft in the pack, with exactly
where to click, for Krish to do.

If a channel shows you signed out, don't sign in. Note "signed out of
<channel>" in the pack's headline and carry on with the public page.

| Channel | Signed-in pages to read | What to take |
|---|---|---|
| Substack | https://home.makeyourmindup.ai/publish/stats (Dashboard, Subscribers, Traffic) | Total, free and paid subscribers; new and lost this day or week; subscribers by source (Recommendations, app, direct, each referrer); views and opens per post |
| YouTube | https://studio.youtube.com (Analytics: Overview, Content, Audience) | Subscribers; views and subscribers gained per video and Short; click-through and average view time per video |
| Instagram | Professional dashboard / Insights for makeyourmind.up | Followers; reach per Reel; shares, saves and sends; profile visits; link taps |
| TikTok | TikTok Studio, Analytics for @therealkrisho | Followers; views per post; average watch time; profile views |
| X | https://x.com/i/account_analytics (or the analytics on each post) | Followers; impressions and profile visits per post; link clicks |
| LinkedIn | Krish's profile analytics and each post's analytics | Followers; impressions per post; profile views; who engaged (job titles) |
| Spotify | https://creators.spotify.com (once the show exists) | Followers; plays; starts; where listeners found it |

For finding conversations (the radar): his LinkedIn feed and search, X
search and lists, Substack's reader feed, YouTube's search. The same rules
apply: read, never act.

## If no signed-in browser is available

Use the public pages (`jobs/numbers.md` lists them) and say in the pack's
headline that the dashboards were not available. Never fill a gap with an
estimate.
