## Where Krish's rules change the documents

Krish's own decisions win over the documents. Each one is recorded here so
no session "fixes" it back:

1. **No Substack Notes for now.** The documents put Notes at the centre of
   the Substack-native engine. Krish, 2026-10-14: "I don't think I need
   Substack notes or WhatsApp to be honest, right now." No job drafts Notes.
   At most once a month, the Sunday report may set out the evidence for
   Notes (a short paragraph with sources). Notes come back only if he says
   so in his own words; record that in `CHANGES.md`.
   **27 October 2026: superseded.** He said so: Substack Notes are back
   (`CHANGES.md`; `docs/plans/2026-10-27-engine-architecture.md`), posted
   from Krish's profile @mindmakerlive at up to two a day as he signs, and
   Threads joins with delegated cadence. Signed exact payloads on X, TikTok,
   Threads and Notes go out through the go-live step; LinkedIn stays his
   click. WhatsApp stays off.
2. **No percentages in anything he reads or posts.** The documents state
   ratios as percentages. This engine states them as counts: "per 1,000
   people reached" or "12 of every 100 visitors".
3. **Agents draft and Krish acts.** The documents assume a team that
   posts, sends, changes settings and runs referral programmes. Here every
   one of those is a draft in a pack, and Krish carries it out.
4. **10,000 in 30 days is a stretch target, never a promise.** The
   documents say the same. The report states real numbers and what they
   imply. It never projects a guess as a forecast.
5. **Editorial belongs to Krish and the content engine.** This engine does
   not choose topics, write articles or change scripts. It measures which
   pieces, hooks and formats bring subscribers, scores each published piece
   (the breakout score), and gives Krish three lines for planning each
   Sunday. Whether to run a 30-day flagship campaign is his call: the report
   may propose one with the evidence.
6. **His time is about 20 minutes a day and 30 on Sunday.** The documents'
   90-minute growth block is the agent's work. Krish's part is to read the
   pack, post and send.
