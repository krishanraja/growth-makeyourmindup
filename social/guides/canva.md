# Canva lane

Ruling: Krish, 27 October 2026 (`CHANGES.md`). Growth makes, in Canva, the
formats content-engine does not cover. Today that is `note` and `meme`, whose
`production_owner` is `growth-canva`. Content-engine keeps its own formats and
standards; this lane applies those standards by reference and never competes.

## Every design

- Use the makeyourmindup brand kit only: its logos, colours and fonts.
- Start from an approved template. A new template needs Krish's approval first.
- Every quote is a literal match to the published piece. Paraphrase is copy,
  not a quote, and is never set in quote marks.
- Where realistic synthetic media is present, apply the destination's native
  AI label where it is supported. Never add illustration boilerplate to public
  copy (`docs/PUBLISHING-AUTHORITY.md`).
- A YouTube meme must be a real video. A still posing as a video is refused.
- Size the export for the selected channel; one meme edition per channel.

## Never

- Never publish, share or schedule from Canva. Canva builds and exports only;
  delivery goes through the route and authority in the channel profile.
- Never edit or delete another brand kit or design.

## Ad-hoc ideas

Krish sends an idea; Growth builds it, shows him the exact export and copy,
and only after his approval queues it for the approved route.

## Where exports go

Approved exports and their manifest go to the Drive 4 Growth library, one
`YYYY-MM-DD - Topic - Channel` folder per campaign, per `docs/ASSET-STORAGE.md`.
The manifest records each file's name, bytes and SHA-256. Candidates and
rejections stay out of that folder. Private evidence stays in `.scratch/`.
