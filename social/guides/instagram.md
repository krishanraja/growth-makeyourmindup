# Instagram: makeyourmind.up publication

Use `../channels/instagram.json` for account and goals. This publication keeps
its clear, useful editorial voice and earned dry humour. It does not need the
personal-account humble-sharing wrapper. Content-engine owns every visual and
narrative production standard; commission there rather than redesigning here.

## Prepare the edition

- Give this edition one explicit discovery, save/share or continuation goal.
  For the first pilot, the practical final rule and save-oriented caption are
  a testable strategy, not a proven engagement mechanism.
- Receive the accepted ordered carousel. Match all image hashes, order, alt text
  and approval identity. Verify cover crop and readable native presentation;
  return any production defect to content-engine.
- Write a caption that adds useful context and preserves the source's limits.
  Keep method/source detail outside the approved artwork. Do not add a generic
  hashtag wall or an unrelated call to action merely to tick a box.
- Only say "link in bio" or promise a Story continuation after opening the
  actual working destination and checking the exact match. Do not assume a
  caption URL or a latest-story profile link provides the promised journey.
- Verify generated-media provenance and applicable native metadata. Reopen the
  saved eight-image draft and confirm the accepted sequence, not the old rejected
  six-image version on the same draft ID.

## Learn before scaling

Record exposed saves, shares, reach and profile/follower movement with dates,
plus source-attributed subscribers where available. Missing metrics stay missing.
Select comparable posts and note topic/audience differences. No result is implied
by a successful upload. Use the native audit for ongoing account health; do not
infer profile, pin or creative-change authority from draft preparation.
