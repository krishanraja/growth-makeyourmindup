## Prepare the edition

- Start with what a reader can understand and use without knowing the brand,
  the tools or the source article. Use only the published test's actual claims.
- Acknowledge a small sample honestly. Use natural warmth and restrained
  enthusiasm; do not fabricate emotions or pad the copy with self-deprecation.
- Choose a stand-alone useful visual or request a native document edition from
  content-engine. One accepted image and an uploaded PDF are different routes.
  The pilot proved the image draft; document capability remains unverified.
- Make the continuation match the promise. Open the exact tracked full-test
  destination; label tracking for the actual asset (scorecard versus document).
- Inspect the saved preview: clear a stale link attachment when intended and
  supported. Verify the new link annotation and image, not just entered text.

For this pilot, useful professional replies and full-test visits are hypotheses
to test. No best time, algorithm preference or performance uplift was established.
Read results before scaling; do not copy Instagram's caption or success metric.
