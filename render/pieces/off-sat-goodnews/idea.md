Five good news clippings let real photographs tell the story before the words. The cover fills the card with one photograph, a centred headline and a small date. An ink gradient keeps the cover words clear. Each story puts an edge-to-edge photograph above a centred headline and one short line. Small house wordmarks brand each card. Thin mint hairlines edge the story panels and sit below the closing wordmark. The centred close points readers to the caption for sources and photo credits.
