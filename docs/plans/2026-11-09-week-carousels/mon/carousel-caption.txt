Millions of people loved Character.AI. It went bust in October. Its founders left for Google in 2024, and now Disney is hiring most of its staff while the lawsuits stay behind.

If your business runs on someone else's AI, four questions about its maker are worth asking this week: are the founders still there, is your tool its main product, is it short of cash, and is it collecting lawsuits?
