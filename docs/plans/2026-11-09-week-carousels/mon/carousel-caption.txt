Your AI app can vanish. Character.AI's users found out in October: Disney is bidding for its staff and code, and the bid leaves out their data and the app.

The warning signs were there two years early: the founders left for Google in 2024, then came lawsuits and a shrinking cash reserve.

If your business runs on someone else's AI, ask your supplier this week what happens to your data and access if it is sold. Get the answer in writing.
