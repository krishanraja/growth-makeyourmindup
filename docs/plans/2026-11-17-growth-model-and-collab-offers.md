## The one rule that keeps the lanes clean

**Signal & Noise gives distribution. Make Your Mind Up takes audience. Mindmake is never the price of either.**

- People who want reach go to **Signal & Noise**: the podcast, The Signal newsletter, Exec Voices. That is what they
  get.
- The cost of a Signal & Noise slot is one thing, stated up front: they share a **Make Your Mind Up companion
  piece** on the same idea with their own audience, carrying a tagged subscribe link. So every person who comes to
  Krish for distribution leaves Make Your Mind Up a little bigger.
- People who already have an audience and don't need ours get a **Make Your Mind Up collaboration** (the ten offers
  below), where we give them something other than reach.
- Mindmake work can be the gift inside a collaboration, but it is never priced, discounted or named as an offer in
  public (Mindmake canon: the price is private; The Teardown and The Signal Session stay retired).

## How to test and learn

**What we're learning:** which offers make people say yes, and which collaborations bring subscribers for the least
of Krish's time. The success measure is new subscribers per collaboration, through its own tagged link, and new
subscribers per hour of Krish's time.

**Round 1, two weeks: which offers get a yes.**
- The Acquisition engine finds three people for each offer (30 in all), chosen for fit and for evidence they say yes
  to collaborations. Each gets exactly one offer, the one that fits them.
- Krish approves every pitch word for word; drafts follow `krish-voice` (outbound email voice: "Hey X, Krish here",
  their own words, one ask, krishraja.com in the sign-off).
- Measure: replies that say yes or ask to hear more, within seven days.
- Decide: an offer with no yes from its three goes; an offer with two or more yeses gets three more pitches in round 2.
  Three is a small sample, so this ranks the offers; it doesn't prove anything.

**Round 2, the next four weeks: which offers bring subscribers.**
- Deliver the first two collaborations for each surviving offer.
- Measure seven days after each one goes out: new subscribers through its link, Krish's hours, and (kept apart) any
  Mindmake conversation it started.
- Decide: keep an offer that clears the bar (proposed: 50 new subscribers per collaboration; Krish sets it) at a
  sensible cost in hours. Kill one that brings fewer than 10 twice.

**Round 3: scale the winner.** Make it a weekly habit: the engine finds targets for the best one or two offers and
Krish approves a batch each week.

**Guardrails.**
- Make Your Mind Up never sells a verdict. Partners never approve what it concludes about them.
- Where Mindmake work or a product was given, the piece says so.
- No price, discount or retired offer name ever appears.
- Every collaboration has its own tagged link before it goes out.
- Acquisition records each one with its offer number, so Measurement can compare offers.
