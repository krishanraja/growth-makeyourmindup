## Outcome

Grow relevant followers and publication subscribers through useful work
and real conversations. Audience fit includes leaders who are themselves
publicly visible on AI's commercial impact. Raw follower totals alone do
not establish audience quality or subscriber attribution.

## Three routines

1. Daily review pack: three LinkedIn opportunities and, when genuinely
   useful, up to two opportunities across the other channels. This stays
   inside the existing shared 20-minute human budget. Target five minutes
   for this engagement pack. Fewer good candidates beat quota fillers.
2. Publishing queue: choose and brief distinct native editions from published
   source stories, receive finished assets from content-engine, then manage
   platform delivery. Read ENGINE-BOUNDARY.md. Production standards and
   creative production learning belong solely to content-engine. Keep
   creative approval and publication authority intact.
   A draft or scheduled asset is not a verified live post.
3. Weekly Codex browser audit: inspect actual profiles and posts, perform
   only authorised existing-field repairs, and verify destinations and
   published results. This remains necessary even if a scheduler is added.

## LinkedIn review pack

Find public AI/commercial discussions from relevant people, prioritising
existing relationships and people whose work Krish has a real reason to
care about. Search results can suggest candidates but do not establish
current post content or permission to prefill a comment.

Before drafting, open the actual post, read its full text and relevant
replies. Record the canonical URL, author, publication time, literal
excerpt, observation time, why it fits, and the exact comment. Distinguish
a top-level comment from a reply to someone else. Do not mine private
messages or claim a personal relationship without evidence.

Default comment: one or two short sentences in Krish's voice. An honest
reaction can stand alone when context makes it specific. An optional
question should be one he would actually want answered. No compulsory
newsletter fact, link, self-promotion, praise template or forced joke.
Never invent experience. Allow a skip without a replacement comment.

Each card shows:

- The post and the relevant passage, with a direct link.
- Why Krish might care, in one sentence.
- The shortest natural comment, plus a question variant only if useful.
- Estimated human time, expiry and state: suggested, ready for review,
  user-confirmed posted, skipped or expired.

The existing radar's 12-hour expiry remains the default. Re-read the live
conversation before preparing an expired replacement. Do not claim a
comment was posted from its draft disappearing or a button click alone.

At handoff, BROWSER.md permits loading one exact post and preparing its
comment box for Krish. Never submit, press Enter or overwrite an existing
human draft. If prefill is unsafe or unavailable, show copy alongside the
post. Current platform rules must be checked; this authority does not
establish that every browser automation technique is permitted by LinkedIn.

## Tool recommendation, researched 2026-10-24

Use Codex for judgment, source research, drafts and live audit. Pilot Buffer
for durable draft queues and approved publishing. Krish has connected the
accounts and the Codex browser draft path is verified. Browser state depends
on an available host, valid session,
reachable tabs and compatible tools. A scheduler is useful infrastructure;
it does not choose good conversations or prove actual channel outcomes.

Buffer's current documentation lists publishing support for Instagram,
TikTok, X, LinkedIn and YouTube Shorts. Long-form YouTube stays native.
Its MCP documents channel/queue/draft access and write operations. On
25 October the native connector verified the intended account, organization,
seven channels and saved draft records, including image dimensions/order
and Instagram alt text. Intermittent transport errors also occurred.
This proves that bounded read path, not reliable unattended publishing or
a deployed repository integration. Recheck availability in each run.

Trial expiry, actual account-specific quotes, billing observations and review
checkpoints live in the ignored private trial ledger. Historical pricing
assumptions are not current quotes. Recheck the actual provider quote before
any paid decision; no plan selection or cancellation is authorised here.

Review on 31 October, prepare the decision on 5 November and make the final
keep/downgrade/stop recommendation on 6 November. Judge native-format
reliability, correctly published approved work, relevant audience growth,
publication conversions and measured human review time. A UTM identifies
placement; it does not prove a subscriber or incremental revenue. Drafts,
impressions and proposed follower targets do not establish financial ROI.
Essentials is the initial candidate for one operator; Team needs evidence
that its additional workflow features earn their extra cost.

Buffer Community is documented as an inbox for comments on owned content,
not arbitrary LinkedIn feed discovery. Its newer Community page lists TikTok
and YouTube, while its channel matrix leaves their engagement columns blank.
Treat exact inbox coverage as a pilot acceptance test, not a purchase premise.
LinkedIn personal-profile analytics are absent from the channel matrix;
native analytics remain necessary. Substack Notes comments are not supported
by Community. Do not route the live paid offer through a new tool.

LinkedIn explicitly targets automated comments and also restricts third-party
website automation. User-posted comments must remain actual human actions.
Draft assistance and a final human click do not automatically validate every
discovery or prefill mechanism. Prefer supported integrations for account
operations; use direct post links plus copy beside them if that boundary
cannot be established. Never sell an autonomous engagement bot as this plan.

Pilot pass: supported formats stay intact; drafts stay drafts; approved
publishing produces one correctly timed post with a native URL; failures
are visible and cannot duplicate a post; revoke/disconnect works; human
review takes at most five minutes; weekly page verification remains intact.
Do not connect all accounts, schedule or publish merely to test capability.
