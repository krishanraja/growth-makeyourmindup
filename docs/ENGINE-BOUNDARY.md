# Growth and production ownership

Ruling: Krish, 26 October 2026. This supersedes any older instruction that
makes Growth a content-production or production-standards owner.

Growth-MakeYourMindUp is the social media manager and distribution engine
for makeyourmindup and Krish's named personal accounts. It ensures published
videos and articles are amplified into suitable native formats and delivered
thoughtfully, strategically and reliably. It optimises useful engagement,
relevant followers, publication subscribers and human effort. Automatic
posting belongs here, under the actual publishing authority granted.

Content-engine is the creator production team. It owns editorial/media
production, production standards, voice execution, visual clarity, narrative
craft, rendering, creative QA and reusable production methods and learning.
Growth commissions finished assets from it; Growth does not run a competing
renderer, creative-standard register or content-production learning system.

| Responsibility | Owner |
|---|---|
| Audience, account roles, channel strategy and cadence | Growth |
| Which existing story to amplify, format mix, where and when | Growth |
| Distribution brief, goal, experiment and continuation | Growth |
| Creative story, copy, design, media adaptation and rendering | Content-engine |
| Production standards, creative QA and production learning | Content-engine |
| Posting package, metadata, links and account targeting | Growth |
| Scheduler, authorised automatic posting and native readback | Growth |
| Live audits, permitted repairs and social account management | Growth |
| Conversation discovery and human engagement preparation | Growth |
| Distribution measurement, experiments and outcome learning | Growth |
| Creative acceptance and publishing authority | Krish under the applicable approval rules |

## Source to distribution

1. Growth identifies the published article/video and canonical identity.
2. Growth briefs content-engine: source links, audience and starting context,
   goal, accounts, native formats, number of editions, timing, experiment,
   practical continuation and observed platform constraints.
3. Content-engine creates and checks the assets through its existing workflow.
   It returns stable references/hashes, order, dimensions/duration, exact
   channel copy, alt text, evidence, unresolved gaps and creative review state.
4. Growth verifies destination fit and approval identity, adds permitted
   platform metadata and queues the package. Content defects return to
   content-engine with specific feedback rather than being redrawn here.
5. Growth schedules/posts through the approved route, reconciles uncertain
   attempts before retrying and verifies the actual native URL, account,
   contents and continuation. A draft or queued item is not a live post.
6. Growth measures comparable outcomes and returns observed creative feedback
   to content-engine. Growth learns distribution; content-engine learns craft.

## Daily social management

For every scoped account, make an explicit daily decision: distribute an
accepted asset, commission the missing edition, prepare a relevant
conversation, audit/repair within authority, learn from results, or hold
with a reason. Use each channel's actual strengths and capabilities.
Cover the publication and the named personal profiles. A connected Buffer
account alone never adds it to scope or permits posting there.

Automatic posting is the delivery goal; its implementation and permissions
must be verified. The existing exact-payload go-live rule remains in force.
This ruling does not approve an unreviewed post or broaden comment, message,
spend, account-setting or paid-offer permissions. LinkedIn engagement remains
a safe human handoff. No claim of live auto-posting from this document.
