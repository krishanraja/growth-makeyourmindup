# Growth and production ownership

Ruling: Krish, 26 October 2026. This supersedes any older instruction that
makes Growth a content-production or production-standards owner.

Growth-MakeYourMindUp is the social media manager and distribution engine
for makeyourmindup and Krish's named personal accounts. It ensures published
videos and articles are amplified into suitable native formats and delivered
thoughtfully, strategically and reliably. It optimises useful engagement,
relevant followers, publication subscribers and human effort. Automatic
posting belongs here, under the actual publishing authority granted.

Content-engine is the creator production team. It owns editorial/media
production, production standards, voice execution, visual clarity, narrative
craft, rendering, creative QA and reusable production methods and learning.
Growth commissions finished assets from it; Growth does not run a competing
renderer, creative-standard register or content-production learning system.

| Responsibility | Owner |
|---|---|
| Audience, account roles, channel strategy and cadence | Growth |
| Which existing story to amplify, format mix, where and when | Growth |
| Distribution brief, goal, experiment and continuation | Growth |
| Creative story, copy, design, media adaptation and rendering | Content-engine |
| Production standards, creative QA and production learning | Content-engine |
| Posting package, metadata, links and account targeting | Growth |
| Scheduler, authorised automatic posting and native readback | Growth |
| Live audits, permitted repairs and social account management | Growth |
| Conversation discovery and human engagement preparation | Growth |
| Distribution measurement, experiments and outcome learning | Growth |
| Creative acceptance and publishing authority | Krish under the applicable approval rules |

## Growth originates the opportunities

Clarification, Krish, 26 October 2026: Growth is proactive. It does not wait
for Krish to request a carousel, choose a new channel or supply a posting
plan. The idea to turn a published piece into another format originates
here, along with the decision about where to propagate it, including useful
placements and channels Krish is not using himself.

Growth owns current channel best practices: native format choice, posting
cadence, captions and links, discovery, engagement tactics, account health,
timing, distribution experiments and the practical plan to gain followers
and engagement across publication and named personal accounts. It researches
and verifies those practices, tests them and improves them from results.
It proposes new placements with a reason and the access/action required;
discovering a channel does not silently authorise creating an account there.

Content-engine owns production standards for ALL formats, not only visual
standards for this carousel. Growth draws on those standards and its existing
production route to execute the selected format at the required quality.
For example, Growth decides whether an article needs a carousel or a video,
which opening to test and where to place it; content-engine governs the
clarity, narrative craft, visual treatment and production QA of that asset.

Channel/posting best practices stay in Growth. Production craft and quality
standards stay in content-engine. Native post packaging and engagement
drafts remain Growth's social-management work, applying the accepted
production/voice standards without inventing a competing canon. Creative
feedback improves content-engine; channel results improve Growth's strategy.

## Source to distribution

Growth's typed channel/format/tool contracts are indexed at social/README.md.
They govern destination fit, package identity, operation and distribution
learning. They reference production standards without reproducing them.

1. Growth identifies the published article/video and canonical identity.
2. Growth briefs content-engine: source links, audience and starting context,
   goal, accounts, native formats, number of editions, timing, experiment,
   practical continuation and observed platform constraints.
   Include the account-speaking context from ACCOUNT-VOICE.md: Krish's
   personal accounts require warm, sincere, humble, useful sharing;
   publication Instagram retains its editorial voice.
3. Content-engine creates and checks the assets through its existing workflow.
   It returns stable references/hashes, order, dimensions/duration, exact
   channel copy, alt text, evidence, unresolved gaps and creative review state.
4. Growth verifies destination fit and approval identity, adds permitted
   platform metadata and queues the package. Content defects return to
   content-engine with specific feedback rather than being redrawn here.
5. Growth schedules/posts through the approved route, reconciles uncertain
   attempts before retrying and verifies the actual native URL, account,
   contents and continuation. A draft or queued item is not a live post.
6. Growth measures comparable outcomes and returns observed creative feedback
   to content-engine. Growth learns distribution; content-engine learns craft.

## Daily social management

For every scoped account, make an explicit daily decision: distribute an
accepted asset, commission the missing edition, prepare a relevant
conversation, audit/repair within authority, learn from results, or hold
with a reason. Use each channel's actual strengths and capabilities.
Cover the publication and the named personal profiles. A connected Buffer
account alone never adds it to scope or permits posting there.

Automatic posting is the delivery goal; its implementation and permissions
must be verified. The existing exact-payload go-live rule remains in force.
This ruling does not approve an unreviewed post or broaden comment, message,
spend, account-setting or paid-offer permissions. LinkedIn engagement remains
a safe human handoff. No claim of live auto-posting from this document.

## 28 October 2026: derived carousels, the off-day lane and the engage queue

Rulings: Krish, 28 October 2026 (`CHANGES.md`), from the plan he approved
that day. For the three things below they supersede the lines above that
keep production out of Growth; content-engine keeps every production
standard and the hero assets of each piece (the article, the two cuts,
thumbnails and covers).

**Growth makes derived carousels.** "take over posting as well as creating
new assets like carousels". Growth makes carousels derived from approved
pieces to content-engine's standard
([content-engine/docs/CAROUSEL-STANDARD.md](https://github.com/krishanraja/content-engine/blob/main/docs/CAROUSEL-STANDARD.md)),
its rejection floor and its copy lint. The standard stays there and is
applied by reference, never copied here. Derived carousels go on the Monday
final board for the same approval as the hero pieces. This supersedes the
26 October split for carousels only.

**The off-day lane is Growth's.** "I also want the Make Your Mind Up
Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts to post on days that are not
mon/weds/fri, with memes, positive uplifting AI news carousels, or simple
valuable advice on how to level up with AI ... the growth repo should be
accountable for this and this should show me quickly for approval, and when
I one click green light it puts it in the next available slot".

- Days and channels: Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday on the
  makeyourmindup Instagram, TikTok (`therealkrisho`) and YouTube Shorts
  channels in the release allowlist. Shorts takes video only, so a carousel
  goes there as a short slideshow.
- Formats: memes made fresh in the brand kit (a borrowed format is
  credited; someone else's image or clip is never reposted); uplifting AI
  news carousels, where every claim names its primary source, is dated
  within 7 days and passes content-engine's fact check before he sees it;
  level-up advice, one practical move per post; and snackable cuts of past
  pieces, named here by word range and cut by content-engine from the
  approved master.
- Planned on Sunday: "I'm happy to plan the Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday,
  and Sunday content within the Sunday ideas session if you give me a few
  options to approve as you do the Mon/wed/fri". Growth feeds 2 to 3
  options a day to the Sunday ideas board through content-engine's
  `week_board.py` inputs, never a board of its own. Each shows its format,
  source and draft copy, and he approves or skips it with one press.
- Mid-week, a green press puts an approved post in the next free off-day
  slot per channel (`docs/PUBLISHING-AUTHORITY.md`).
- Accountable: Growth reports slots filled against slots available, and
  per-format results, to Control Center.

**Engagement is a daily 10-minute queue.** "10 min daily queue is fine, add
Instagram and LinkedIn to this, and make sure everything is one click
copiable and the click through goes precisely where i need to engage."
Growth prepares it across X, TikTok, Instagram and LinkedIn: each reply
drafted with a one-click copy button, each link opening the exact post,
reply box or profile. Control Center shows it. It stays prepared only: he
submits every reply, and following and liking stay his.

Built so far (28 October): the release channel lock and the copy lint. The
lane, the engage queue, derived carousels and the Monday go-live reads are
Wave 2 of the plan, with the first off-day posts due Tuesday 10 November.
