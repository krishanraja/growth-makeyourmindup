18 of 23 read: 3 likes in all, no replies. Subscriber effect: not measurable per comment, and the week's subscriber
read has not run.

What it means: comments under big media accounts earned almost no attention, which is consistent with the reorientation
(`2026-11-17-reorientation.md`): borrowed audiences come from partners and Substack's own network, not from replies
under news accounts. Recommendation for Krish: stop comments under media accounts; keep, at most, a few replies on
business-AI writers' Notes, judged by subscribers.
