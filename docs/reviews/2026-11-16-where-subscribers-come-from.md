# Where Make Your Mind Up's subscribers come from, and where the effort goes now (16 Nov 2026)

Krish, 16 Nov 2026: "I need you to act like a $200,000 employee not an intern."

Read today from the Substack dashboard (Growth sources, Network, Recommendations, post stats, Traffic) by a
read-only Codex session on the worker ("MYMU: where the 497 subscribers came from (16 Nov 2026)"); raw report
in the ignored `.scratch/paid/run16-10-sources.log`. Counts are Substack's own; where its views disagree, both are
given. Inferences are marked as such.

## What the numbers say

1. **The list is shrinking.** 523 subscribers 90 days ago, 497 today (-26). About 33 people joined in that time,
   so about 59 left. The 30-day email open rate is 21.6%.
2. **Half the list was imported, not earned.** 254 of 496 accounts are "Imported accounts"; 266 sign-ups are
   labelled "Import". *Inference, unverified:* most of the leavers and most of the unopened emails sit in that
   imported half. Unsubscribes by source were not read.
3. **New readers come from inside Substack.** In the last 90 days, 23 of 27 new accounts (85%) already had a
   Substack account. That is the Substack network: Notes, recommendations, the app.
4. **Nobody recommends us.** 0 publications recommend Make Your Mind Up. We recommend three (Nate's Substack, GTM
   Vault, AI Realist, since 11 Nov); none has sent a subscriber or recommended us back.
5. **Social and paid sent almost nobody.** In 90 days: Instagram 244 views and Facebook 240 views (mostly while
   the boosts ran), 1 subscriber each. LinkedIn 16 views, 2 subscribers. X has never appeared as a source. 139
   posts and about $120 plus £13.90 of boosts in six weeks brought about 2 subscribers.
6. **"Direct" is the biggest source of new people** (21 of 33 in 90 days): links opened with no referrer, which is
   what messaging apps, email forwards and the LinkedIn app produce. *Inference:* this is mostly Krish's own
   network.
7. **One kind of piece converts ten times better.** "Learnings from an AI Chief of Staff" (16 Mar): 5 subscribers
   from 70 views, about 7%. The October video posts: 1 to 2 from 150 to 250 views, under 1%. Small numbers, so a
   signal, not proof: the first-person, specific piece out-converted the explainer videos.
8. **Unexplained:** 86 views from "google-paid" in the last 90 days. Nothing in this engine ran Google ads.

## The gap, honestly

The last 90 days brought about 33 new subscribers from about 1,900 non-email views (direct, social, search, app):
about 1.7 per 100. At that rate, 162 a week needs about 9,500 views a week from the right people. The last 90 days
averaged about 145 a week. That is roughly a sixty-fivefold gap.
No fix to captions, bios or posting times closes it. Only borrowed audiences at scale can: other writers'
readers, Krish's own network, and Substack's network.
