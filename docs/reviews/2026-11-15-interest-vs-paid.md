**3. Reach without interest, almost certainly paid or a feed test.**
- TikTok, "The UK number two" (11 Nov): 17,991 views, 65 times the channel median, with 16 reactions, 1 share and
  4.3 s average watch: about 1 reaction per 1,000 views, against a median of 10. The same story on Instagram, with
  no such reach, earned 4 shares and 28.7 s of watching. The story interests people; the TikTok reach did not.
- YouTube, the same story: 4,805 views and 0 reactions; "When an AI agent gets a computer" 1,166 views, 5
  reactions. YouTube's median is 21.
- Facebook, "When an AI agent gets a computer and no instructions" (13 Nov): reach 8,984 against a page median of
  2, with 66 reactions, 5 shares and 10 clicks (8 per 1,000). A Page with a median reach of 2 does not reach 8,984
  people unpaid, so this is very likely a boost. No boost is in this repository's records, which still say "no
  paid boosts" (`NOW.md`): **the record and the readings disagree**, a reconciliation finding until Codex reads
  the ads managers.
- Whether any of this reach brought a subscriber is unknown: the week of 9 to 15 Nov has not been read from
  Substack yet.

**What is worth paying to boost: nothing proven, and it is outside the North Star.** The likely boosts bought
large reach with the lowest engagement per view of any post on their channels; the organically shared posts are
the ones with an audience. If paid is kept at all, it is a test with its own question (does paid reach bring
subscribers at a known cost?), reported apart, and only Krish can change the zero-paid line.
