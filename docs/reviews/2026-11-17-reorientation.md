# Reorientation: the audience Krish already has, and partners who bring theirs (17 Nov 2026)

Krish shared his 17 Nov LinkedIn chat with Boardy, the AI connector that makes his introductions, and asked this
engine to "reorientate to the reality we need to address". This replaces the lever ranking in
`2026-11-16-where-subscribers-come-from.md`; its numbers still stand.

## Krish's lanes, in his words

"I'm happy to have people that just want to use me for distribution on SignalandNoise.ai because that's a bit more
established. But when it comes to Make Your Mind Up I need distribution partners right now." Make Your Mind Up's
partners are "prominent speakers with big reach ... [who] want me for my style, the topic, and the pitch that I would
build with them (because I am a great host and moderator)". Then: "Pitching them: make your mind up so that I can
borrow their audience." The corporate content programme is "a companion pitch alongside MindMake", so it belongs to
Mindmake's lane, not this engine's.

## The reality

1. **Make Your Mind Up owns about 600 people.** 497 Substack subscribers (the public profile shows 496, which anyone
   can check) and about 100 social followers. Krish told Boardy "about 700"; any pitch should use the number a
   partner can verify.
2. **Krish already reaches about 19,000 more, and almost none of them have been pointed at it.** His counts, given
   to Boardy and not yet verified here: LinkedIn about 5,000, Maven about 6,000, Signal & Noise about 8,000
   listeners, with little overlap. Substack shows maven.com has sent 1 subscriber ever, LinkedIn 2, and Signal &
   Noise has never appeared as a source. The content canon already gives Maven and Signal & Noise the job of feeding
   the publication (`content-corpus`, `editorial-channels.md` and `maven-and-outbound.md`). This is the largest
   untouched pool by two orders of magnitude.
3. **Reach is not the pitch; craft is.** Boardy, and our own evidence agree: nobody with reach moves for 600, and
   reach without the right reader brings no subscribers (the pop song, the boosts). The offer is a produced show
   with the partner's name on it, put in front of Krish's four audiences, and the partner shares it with theirs.
   The partners who say yes have something landing in the next 60 to 90 days. Start at our size or a notch up,
   measure it, then go bigger with the result.

## Decisions only Krish can make

1. **The vehicle for partner episodes.** Signal & Noise has the listeners but is co-hosted (Rio Longacre and Brett
   House at the last confirmed refocus) and is the lane for people who want his distribution. Make Your Mind Up's
   own YouTube is his but has almost no audience. Recommended: record once, publish where the listeners are, and
   make the page the partner shares a Make Your Mind Up piece that carries the subscribe link, so their audience
   lands on this engine's door.
2. **The bar for a good outcome.** Boardy asked. Proposed for this lane: a collaboration whose shared link brings at
   least 50 new subscribers.
3. **Audience fit for the 1 Dec launch.** The book is subtitled "Artificial Intelligence for Everyday Life" and
   pitched at beginners; Make Your Mind Up's reader is a senior commercial leader. Worth doing for the first named
   collaboration and its reach, but judged on subscribers, not views.
