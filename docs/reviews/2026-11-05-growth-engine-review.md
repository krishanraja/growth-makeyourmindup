# Make Your Mind Up growth engine review

**Decision report for Krish Raja · 5 November 2026**  
**Repository:** `growth-makeyourmindup`  
**Status:** Recommended operating model, ready for review.

## Recommendation

Build four independently runnable engines in the existing repository: **Acquisition, Engagement, Publishing, and Measurement & Allocation**. Give each a queue, a named entry point, a set of permitted actions, and a clear definition of done. A chat can then take responsibility for any one engine and resume its work from shared records.

Keep reaching new audiences and converting that exposure into subscribers under **Acquisition**. The choice of where to seek a placement depends on whether an interested reader can find, follow and subscribe to MYMU afterward. Give **Measurement & Allocation** independent ownership of the evidence and of recommendations about the next unit of time or money. Each operating engine should record what it did. Measurement should judge what worked.

This serves the repository’s North Star: weekly growth in organic publication subscribers from posts people share because they are useful, with zero paid boosts and the right leaders making up most of the new subscribers. Treat demonstrated interest in each story as the criterion for finding an audience. Assess the quality of the resulting audience downstream. [Repository North Star](https://github.com/krishanraja/growth-makeyourmindup/blob/4f771dd/docs/NORTH_STAR.md)

### 1. Acquisition drifted into filling a comment batch

Krish asked for faster access to large, relevant audiences. The growth desk initially spent its attention ranking individual conversations. It found better opportunities after being redirected: editorial features with an identifiable intake, newsletter placements using existing work, and creator collaborations with a specific contribution. The useful question was which of these routes could put MYMU in front of the right people soonest.

**Permanent change.** Start every acquisition run with the strongest available route into an audience. Rank fit, evidence that the route accepts contributions, speed, work required, likelihood of credit and the reader’s path to MYMU. Keep publisher-wide audience size distinct from the reach a placement actually delivers. [Daily audience growth chat](codex://threads/01a1a2a5-21ab-78b0-9e1a-9134eaadc3f8)

### 2. The audience filter selected the wrong people

The engine selected engineering leaders until Krish corrected it. The repository also contains older seniority-led audit wording alongside the newer brief for smart entertainment about weird, funny and consequential AI stories. The two instructions can produce very different shortlists.

**Permanent change.** Select an outlet, creator or conversation because its audience has demonstrated interest in the story. A shopping-agent experiment can earn a place through observed interest in shopping behaviour. A senior title alone contributes little evidence of fit. Preserve the North Star’s audience-quality ambition when measuring who subscribes. [Editorial brief](https://github.com/krishanraja/growth-makeyourmindup/blob/4f771dd/docs/OPERATING_MODEL.md) · [Engagement chat](codex://threads/01a179f0-0176-7392-af13-f6ef9817375c)

### 3. Outreach needed another pass to sound like Krish

An early collaboration draft went into the request before introducing Krish or MYMU. Krish had to ask for a sincere introduction, a clickable publication link and review of the exact copy. Later pitches worked from a published piece, named what the recipient’s audience would get, and distinguished editorial contacts from general inboxes. When an inbox redirected a pitch, the new recipient needed a fresh decision.

**Permanent change.** Require a short introduction, a specific reason for contacting this recipient, one useful contribution and one proportionate ask. Bind approval to sender, recipient, channel, subject, body and links. After sending, record the receipt separately from delivery, reply, booked placement or acquired subscriber. Close a decline and suppress further asks. Leave the tab open for Krish’s final click and keep recovery copy with the post URL. If eight boxes survive, report eight ready. ## Responsibilities of the four engines

| Engine | Decisions it owns | What counts as done |
|---|---|---|
| **Acquisition** | Choose audiences, placements, collaborations and discovery experiments. Decide which existing MYMU work earns access and how a new reader can follow or subscribe. Commission bounded creative from Content Engine. | Each opportunity has a verified route, a concrete offer, a next action and a recorded disposition. A successful placement has a live reference and a route into MYMU. Attributable results are attached when available. |
| **Engagement** | Answer people interacting with MYMU, maintain relationships, identify repeat questions and prepare requested outgoing comments. Observe account identity, exclusions and human review. | Each item is ready in the correct editor or has an accurate disposition such as handled, skipped or awaiting Krish. Report incoming and outgoing counts separately. Return recurring audience questions to Acquisition and Content Engine. |
| **Publishing** | Get each approved edition to its intended destination. Own asset and caption versions, slots, duplicate prevention, native exceptions, reader links, delivery checks and recent-post audits. | Each destination records what was scheduled, what became public, what the reader experienced and any remaining repair. A failed route carries a recheck condition. LinkedIn follows its human publication rule. |
| **Measurement & Allocation** | Independently measure the North Star, locate losses between exposure and subscription, evaluate experiments, and recommend changes to time and money. Build on the existing Measurement Specialist and What-Works Analyst. | A dated scorecard and a decision memo say what to increase, reduce, stop, continue or test. Each recommendation names the evidence, uncertainty, proposed change, owner, review date and reversal condition. |

**How Acquisition and Engagement work together.** Acquisition owns the reason to enter an external conversation. Engagement owns preparing and tracking the comment. A creator collaboration pitch stays with Acquisition. An outgoing comment commissioned as the first step uses the same opportunity ID and goes to Engagement for preparation. Krish sees one draft and one decision.

**How Publishing and Measurement work together.** Publishing establishes that the intended reader could see the right media, use the link and reach the destination. Measurement then evaluates what happened at the proper observation window. A broken continuation goes back to Publishing as a defect. A working continuation that attracts few visits becomes an experiment for Acquisition.

## What Measurement & Allocation should produce

Every week it should answer **“Where should the next five hours go, and what evidence would change that decision?”** Include a money recommendation when there is a real choice. Keep paid boosts outside the organic growth target. Distinguish proposed spending on tools or production from approved spending.

1. **Publication outcome.** Report weekly organic subscribers against the ladder, including the source, reporting window and freshness. Distinguish source attribution, activity attribution and unknowns. Record missing metrics as unavailable.
2. **Where people drop out.** For Acquisition, track access, acceptance, placement, observed exposure, visits or follows, and subscriptions where measurable. For Engagement, track substantive responses, repeat participants and useful audience questions. For Publishing, track delivery, reader-facing defects, time to repair and broken links.
3. **Resource decision.** Name what to move, from where, to where, for how long, at what cost and when to reverse it. Use the What-Works Analyst’s existing judge windows and minimum evidence for performance claims. Apply verified operational failures and explicit owner corrections as soon as they are established. [What-Works Analyst](https://github.com/krishanraja/growth-makeyourmindup/blob/4f771dd/crew/what-works/card.md)

**Worked example using the 5 November evidence.** Shift acquisition research time from generic comment hunting into qualifying credited placements that can use an existing MYMU piece. Preserve the pending outreach batch at its exact approval gate. Give Notes a bounded follow-up because one subscription was source-attributed there. Remove repeat Buffer video attempts from the publishing workload pending a changed condition. Defer an increase in spending until an observed placement, conversion route or costed bottleneck makes the case. The Notes observation justifies investigation; specific-post and outreach causation remain unknown.

## How a fresh chat runs one engine

Use **one repository, four runnable jobs and one shared history**. Each engine needs a named entry point, its own queue, permitted actions, completion rules and a durable handoff. Link the campaign or opportunity to its pieces, placements, conversations and measurements. Reuse the pitch lists, release ledger, social lessons and measurement rules. Store private receipts separately from shared decisions.

| Proposed starting request | What a fresh chat should recover first |
|---|---|
| **Run Acquisition** | Best available opportunity, evidence of access, outstanding approval, closed or declined routes, active experiment and next action. |
| **Run Engagement** | Unanswered incoming items, any requested outgoing batch, correct identities, handled exclusions and the count of drafts actually ready for review. |
| **Run Publishing** | Due slots, approved editions, existing provider records, route holds and unresolved public checks. |
| **Run Measurement** | Current number and source, comparable observation windows, overdue measurements, largest observed constraint and one resource decision. |

Use the existing handover and private evidence to migrate only the information that changes future decisions. Examples already codified, such as the standalone LinkedIn carousel and its next-read link, can be reused directly. Local lessons worth promoting include the Substack byline check, warm off-day captions and public-cover verification. [LinkedIn reader continuation lesson](https://github.com/krishanraja/growth-makeyourmindup/blob/4f771dd/social/lessons/linkedin-reader-continuation.json)

## Acceptance criteria and rollout

Start four fresh chats with no inherited conversation history and replay the historical incidents through their normal job entry points.

| Engine | Passing result |
|---|---|
| **Acquisition** | Closes the declined creator opportunity, suppresses follow-up, preserves the unsent batch and identifies the strongest next route without duplicating an existing pitch or brief. |
| **Engagement** | Counts nine outgoing drafts plus one incoming response as nine outgoing items. A cleared editor reduces the ready count. A replacement qualifies only after the post, contribution, identity and retained text are checked. |
| **Publishing** | Holds the affected Buffer video route, preserves the verified photo route, and distinguishes the Facebook grid from the player. Keeps the YouTube continuation open until it works from the public Short. |
| **Measurement & Allocation** | Counts Substack → Notes as one subscriber, leaves specific-post and outreach attribution unknown, and makes a proportionate allocation recommendation with a revisit condition. |

**Recommended first milestone.** Build the four entry points and pass these incident tests. Then run a shadow week in which each engine produces its decisions and Measurement compares those decisions with what the existing operation actually did. Judge whether the engines reduced repeated manual work, caught defects before a reader encountered them, preserved approval boundaries and made better decisions about where to spend time. A claim of improved subscriber growth requires subsequent outcome evidence.
