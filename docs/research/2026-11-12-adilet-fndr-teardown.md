# Competitor teardown: @adilet.fndr (Instagram), 12 November 2026

Krish asked on 12 November 2026 how this account grew so fast ("I want to know exactly what he's doing"). Public data only: his 36 public reels, his profile and his own sales page, which shows his Instagram dashboard screenshots. Read on 12 November 2026 through Firecrawl (page reads and the ScrapeCreators reels feed), about 17 credits. No account was followed, commented on or messaged.

## The call

His growth is real and it is a power law. Eight reels brought about 89% of his net follows, and one hook run three times brought 62%. Around them sits a system that makes those reels cheap to produce and turns their reach into money: a comment-keyword DM funnel, an email capture and a $67 product.

## The mechanism

1. **A narrow, rising audience.** People building apps with Claude Code ("vibe coders") and solo founders, as Claude Code took off.
2. **Hooks with money on the line.** A dollar figure, a consequence and a "before you" warning ("$100K before a single sale", "running at 0/4 power", "don't start until these 4 are in"), then a dense numbered list of specifics (COPPA, Google Fonts and a Munich court ruling, VAT).
3. **He re-runs winners.** The same hook, reworded, three times in ten days, each a hit.
4. **Every post is a checklist people keep.** His top reel's screenshot shows 29K, 5.7K, 932, 25.1K and 72.4K. Read as likes, comments, reposts, shares and saves (the order is inferred, not labelled), about 5.6% of viewers saved it.
5. **Comment a keyword, get a DM.** PROMPT, YC, OPUS, SKILL, CHAT and FABLE. On those reels comments run at about 3.5 times likes (12,840 comments against 3,702 likes on one). The DM sends people to adiletfndr.com, which asks for an email before checkout ("Where should I send your skill?"), then to a $67 Gumroad product. The keyword lifts the reel and builds the list at once. The DM tool is not visible; an automation is inferred.
6. **Near-zero production cost.** 12 reels in his first 12 days, 35 in 54 days, all faceless and AI-animated, made by a Claude Code skill that writes, voices (ElevenLabs), animates and checks each reel from one line.
7. **Money.** The $67 skill, brand deals he puts at "$2.5k+" (Higgsfield, #HiggsfieldCreator; Abacus AI ChatLLM) and affiliate links sent through the keyword DMs.

Since 24 October he has turned from useful tips to "how I make these reels", to sell the product. Those reels convert views to follows at about half the rate of the lawsuit hook (0.49% against 1.25%), and his growth slowed from about 1,050 followers a day to about 770.

## What makeyourmindup takes, and what it does not

- **Take: the keyword-to-DM-to-subscribe loop.** It is the one mechanic that turns views into Substack subscribers rather than followers. Test plan: `docs/plans/2026-11-13-keyword-dm-test.md`.
- **Take: one hook, re-run.** Our closest is follow.the.money: a specific sum, a specific person, a date.
- **Take: posts people save.** Numbered, concrete, a checklist.
- **Take: daily volume through programmatic production.** Content-engine already makes video in code.
- **Do not take: fear.** His hooks run on threat ("you'll get sued"). Krish's enemy is "fear and noise", and a buyer whose company pays never sees fear framing. Keep the stakes and the numbers; drop the threat.

## Limits of this evidence

- His follows per reel and his dashboard totals are his own screenshots on a page that sells his product. The independent play counts read for this teardown agree with their size.
- His audience is a mass market of builders. Senior commercial leaders are a smaller pool and share less, so the same moves should be expected to yield smaller numbers.
- Followers are not subscribers. His real conversion figure, email sign-ups, is not public.
