Make Your Mind Up chain. Plan of 28 October 2026, amended 29 October. Wave 2 lands by **Saturday 7 November 2026**, and its first real use is **Sunday 8 November**.

## What Wave 2 is for

Wave 1 made the Sunday loop work. Krish presses on one board, scripts go to Teleprompter, the words gate holds a cut that loses a word, and no public word names him. Production still runs attended, on quick-edit.

Wave 2 makes the week run without a person minding it:

- the cuts are made overnight on his home computers;
- calibrated machine gates prove each cut before he sees it;
- the board keeps every past piece with his scores;
- Control Center tells him what is late and whose job it is;
- Growth schedules exactly what he approved, and runs the off-day lane.

On Sunday 8 November the overnight run produces the week of 16 November on quick-edit plus the calibrated gates. `ve` runs in shadow on the same takes, so the two can be compared side by side.

### W2.4 Gates that are calibrated (content-engine)

Calibration comes first. These cuts must pass:
- Monday 26's accepted long and short cuts.

These cuts must fail:
- Wednesday 21's published long cut;
- Friday's rejected join reel;
- Friday's 90-second cut made from the 17 October words file.

**The words gate.** From advisory to blocking on both routes, now calibrated.
- A content word lost on both aligners' passes fails the cut, naming the word.
- Function words are listed, never blocking.

**Joins in gaps.** Every join sits in a gap both aligners agree on (at least 80 ms, with 30 ms clearance), which catches clipped word tails.

**Captions, in two parts:**
- **Clock truth:** the finished audio is re-aligned with the aligner that did not draw the captions. Each caption page's first frame is held to it, within the tolerance measured on Monday 26.
- **Renderer truth:** caption pages are detected from pixel changes in the caption zone and read by OCR, only within plus or minus 3 frames of each page start. The text must match the props exactly, and score at least 0.98 by OCR.

**Second aligner.** ElevenLabs Scribe, after a one-file check of its word timestamps and his yes on its key and spend.

**Receipts.** Written by the machine and bound to the file's hash. The self-attested `not_run` fields go.

**Each cut's joins reel** goes on the Monday board.

**Acceptance:**
- All five calibration cuts behave as listed above.
- Mutation tests fail as they should: a deleted content word, a join moved into speech, captions drawn from a stale clock.

**Krish:** his yes on the ElevenLabs key and spend, or the gates run on faster-whisper alone.
