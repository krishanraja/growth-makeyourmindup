# Wave 3: `ve` goes live, inspiration in one place, the repos tidied, the retrospective

Make Your Mind Up chain. Plan of 28 October 2026, amended 29 October. Wave 3 lands by **Saturday 14 November 2026**. Its first real use is **Sunday 15 November**, the earliest week `ve` produces live.

## What Wave 3 is for

Wave 2 makes the week run unattended on quick-edit, with `ve` in shadow. Wave 3 has five aims:

- move production to `ve` once it has proved itself side by side with quick-edit;
- give inspiration one home, with stills that travel;
- tidy the local and Drive sprawl, only with his consent;
- start the retrospective that learns from his scores;
- grow the visual range one or two devices a week.

## The rules this wave must keep

His words, in force for every item below. They are the same as in Wave 2:

- **Taste:** the eight floor nevers are the only taste blocks. Aims guide and never block. Choices under test are recorded and judged on engagement. Everything else is an example of the kind of thing that does not fly, never a new rule.
- **No Claude routines:** GitHub Actions on his subscription, or Codex and scripts on his home computers.
- **Cost:** timed API calls stay under $15 a week.
- **Standing walls:** nothing publishes, sends, spends, deletes or changes a permission without his yes; `ve` pull requests are merged by hand after green CI; filearchives never moves a folder without his consent row.

### W3.6 The retrospective (inspirationhouse, Control Center)

**A `retro` stage in inspirationhouse.** It runs monthly, and after every 4 newly scored pieces, on the Control Center clock (code, not a Claude routine).

**What it does:**
- joins his scores (`piece_scores`, Wave 2) and engagement to each piece's features: its narrative, its production, and the choices under test (the opening, the lead picture, the big-moment sound);
- sends text claims with citations as proposals, which only he approves;
- shapes form and production, never topic ranking (the anti-echo rule).

**Acceptance:** its first report names, with evidence, one thing that scored well and one that scored badly, and makes no claim beyond the data.

**Krish:** the backfill scores from Wave 2, and his reading of the first report.
