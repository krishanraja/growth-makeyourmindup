reference is a newsletter welcome that says what you get and when in a few plain lines, jokes at the expense of
the hype rather than the reader, and ends on one easy ask. Use that for rhythm only. Do not copy its words or
its promises. All the words you need are below.

## Hard rules

1. Read freely. Change a setting or a piece of copy only after Krish says yes to that exact change in this
   session, immediately before you make it. Show him the current text and the new text side by side first. One
   yes covers one surface.
2. Never send a post, email, DM, Note or comment to anyone. The only email you may send is Substack's own
   "send test email" to Krish's own address, and only after he says yes to it.
3. Never spend money or start a paid feature or trial. Never change a price, a plan, a benefit or a setting that
   is not named here.
4. No em dashes in anything you type into Substack. If the editor inserts one, remove it and tell Krish. British
   spelling.
5. No person is named in publication copy, and no face. Never write Krish's name into anything.
6. Do not invent a number, a customer, a result or a date. Do not edit a number that is already in the copy.
7. Jokes point at hype, launch videos, jargon, the situation or the makeyourmindup team. Never at the reader, a
   subscriber, or a named person or company. No closing moral, no advice to the reader, no line that sounds like a
   conference slide. Plain words only: a stranger must understand every line.
8. If a page looks different from what this brief expects, report what you saw with a screenshot. Do not guess
   the menu path.
9. Out of scope, do not touch: the Instagram keyword DM, any Mindmake page, any email in Krish's Gmail, the
   subscription benefits lists, pricing, and the post archive.

Wednesday, under.the.hood. We take apart something that shipped and stamp every part real or theatre.
